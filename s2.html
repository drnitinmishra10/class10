Nutrition
Q1
2 Marks
The mode of nutrition in Amoeba is:
A. Saprophytic
B. Holozoic
C. Autotrophic
D. Parasitic
Amoeba follows holozoic nutrition where it ingests food using pseudopodia.
Q2
2 Marks
Which enzyme is present in saliva?
A. Pepsin
B. Trypsin
C. Lipase
D. Salivary amylase
Salivary amylase breaks starch into simpler sugars in the mouth.
Q3
2 Marks
The inner lining of the small intestine has finger-like projections called:
A. Nephrons
B. Alveoli
C. Villi
D. Stomata
Villi increase the surface area for absorption of digested food.
Q4
2 Marks
Which of the following is essential for photosynthesis?
A. Chlorophyll
B. Oxygen
C. Protein
D. Nitrogen
Chlorophyll traps sunlight needed for photosynthesis.
Q5
2 Marks
Assertion (A): Bile juice helps in digestion of fats.
Reason (R): Bile juice contains enzymes that digest fats.
A. Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation
B. A is true but R is false
C. A is false but R is true
D. Both A and R are false

Respiration
Q6
2 Marks
Which gas is released during respiration?
A. Nitrogen
B. Oxygen
C. Carbon dioxide

Q7
2 Marks
The tiny air sacs in lungs are called:
A. Nephrons
B. Bronchi
C. Trachea
D. Alveoli

Q8
2 Marks
During vigorous exercise, muscle cells may undergo:
A. Anaerobic respiration
B. Photosynthesis
C. Digestion
D. Excretion

Q9
2 Marks

A. Fermentation
B. Digestion
C. Aerobic respiration
D. Diffusion

Q10
2 Marks

Reason (R): Tar affects alveoli and decreases respiratory efficiency.
A. A false, R true
B. Both true and R explains A
C. Both true but R not explanation
